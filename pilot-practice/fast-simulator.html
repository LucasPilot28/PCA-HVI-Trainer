FAST Flight Simulator

Practice multitasking mission in the FAST style, followed by a twelve-question debrief.

Back up or restore your results

Your attempts are stored in this browser only. Copy the text below into a note or file to keep it. To restore it later (or on another device), paste it into the box and press Restore.

Controls

  • ↑ ↓ or W S: steer the plane in the left screen around the white bars. Any part of the plane touching a bar, wings and tail included, counts as a crash.
  • Aircraft: press each time you see aircraft outside the canopy.
  • Waypoint: press as you fly over each waypoint ATC has named. Nobody tells you when you pass it, and the route map has no live position: it only moves on to a waypoint once you have flown over it or pressed Waypoint correctly, so judge it from the ground and the timing.
  • Click the answers in the right screen. Each question shows for a few seconds, then its options. A cockpit voice reads each instruction aloud; it is not one of the radio voices.

Keep track of

The debrief asks about things nobody tells you to count: aircraft seen, waypoint names, voices you heard (radio and question reader), red light, beeps, where the helicopters and the airfield were, the final waypoint, and what the cockpit looked like.

Set-up

Use a computer with headphones and the sound on. ATC is read by your browser's speech voices. If none are available, captions switch on by themselves. The score bands are my own practice guide, not official FAST norms.
